The agent, the simulated user and the judge each have their own limit on work at once, and each agent can have its own. Set them under `[tool.convy]` in `pyproject.toml`, or with `CONVY_AGENT_PARALLEL` and the like for one run. The judge no longer holds a conversation's place, `convy resume` can go on with other limits, and `convy compare` says when two runs had different agent limits.
