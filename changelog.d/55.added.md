Add required claims and `--claim-threshold` to score each attempt against all its claims; low-confidence decisions now fail their claims instead of being excluded, and reports show completion and why an attempt failed. This is a breaking change: Python scenarios take `Requirement` records and use `Scenario.score` instead of `Verdict.passed` or `Verdict.decided`, exports add `claims.required` and `runs.claim_threshold`, and only run format 8 is supported without conversion.
