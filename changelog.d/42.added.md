The agent, the simulated user and the judge can each keep to a limit of requests per minute: `agent_per_minute`, `user_per_minute` and `judge_per_minute` under `[tool.convy]`, or `per_minute` in an agent's own table. convy spaces their requests evenly, so a known limit meets almost no 429; `convy compare` and `convy resume` say when the agent's limit per minute changed.
