convy waits for a busy service by time, up to 2 minutes, not by a count of tries: at least as long as it asks, with a random delay so that refused requests do not come back together. While a service is busy, convy sends it fewer requests at once and goes back up as it answers. An agent whose service stays busy has no verdict and no longer lowers the pass rate, and waiting is not counted in the time per answer.
