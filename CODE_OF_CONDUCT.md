# Code of Conduct

## Respectful participation

Treat people with respect, regardless of their background, identity or experience.
Welcome questions, give useful feedback, and discuss ideas and code without
attacking people. Respect requests to stop unwanted contact.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment and sharing someone's
private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope

These rules apply in convy's issues, pull requests and other project spaces, and
when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and response

Report violations privately to the maintainer at
[literallybugcreator@gmail.com](mailto:literallybugcreator@gmail.com).
Include what happened and relevant links; do not post private details in an issue.

The maintainer will review reports fairly and share details only with those who
need them to respond. Depending on the severity, responses may include removing
content, a warning, or a temporary or permanent restriction from project spaces.
Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is not acceptable.
